## What was tested

- Fresh visitors on new origins (no saves), at phone width (375 px) and desktop.
- **Owner account** (`themoojuice`, 1,571 records): import and reveal in ~28 s with no errors.
- **HANDOVER §10 smoke test** plus the v2.0 and v2.6 modals (honour card, dispatch, art tagger): 32 of 32 pass, no console errors,
  no horizontal page scroll on any tab. The only wide elements are the intended sideways strips and the Ladder table, which scrolls inside its panel.
- **Guest mode with a US home** (Austin, TX): Three to find (*Phidippus*, *Anasaitis*, *Hentzia*), weather in °F, scanner, "0 / 103 genera on iNat in United States".
- **A pet keeper** (a real US account whose only salticid record is captive).
- **A mistyped username**, and a username typed with `@` and capitals.
- **Display case link** (`?u=themoojuice&case`) opened by a stranger.
- **Album** and a genus page with field art.
- Load: one new visitor makes about 40–70 throttled iNat calls in their first few minutes. Every call comes from that visitor's own
  browser and IP, so a Reddit spike adds no shared quota or server.

Not tested: real iPhone Safari (most Reddit traffic is mobile and much of it is iOS), the print dialog, and a real 429 from iNat.

## Fix before posting (small, high impact)

1. **Link preview.** Neither `index.html` nor `spoodex.html` has `og:`/`twitter:` tags or a meta description. `/spoodex/` is a
   JavaScript redirect, and Reddit's scraper only reads `index.html`. Today a link post shows "SPOODEX" with no description and no image.
   Fix: add `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image` (absolute URL, 1200×630, for example sticker art on kraft with a few cards),
   `og:url` and `twitter:card=summary_large_image` to **both** files, plus the image as a new approved sidecar.
2. **Pet keepers see a misleading message.** r/jumpingspiders is mostly pet keepers. A keeper whose records are all captive gets
   "Level 0 · No public jumping spider records identified to genus yet", which is wrong: they have one, and it's captive. The model already
   knows (`o.cap`). Say it, for example: "1 captive record not counted: SPOODEX collects spiders found in the wild." Add a "find a wild one
   near you" path into guest-style Three to find. This tells the user how to act, so it isn't filler.
3. **A mistyped username** shows "Couldn't finish: iNaturalist returned 422 for /observations". iNat's body says `Unknown user_id <name>`.
   Show "No iNaturalist user called *x*. Use your login name (it's in your profile URL), not your display name." Expect display names and
   emails in that box.
4. **Weather stars saturate.** Austin in October rated ★★★★★ on all 7 days (Cairns rates ★4 all week in dry spells, §12). If every day gets
   five stars, the stars don't help anyone choose a day. Either rank days within the week or raise the ★5 bar (`WX.stars`).

## Worth doing soon

- **Display case cold start**: a stranger waits ~15–20 s on "Read 1,200 of 1,571 records" before the case draws. If the Reddit post links to
  your case, which I'd recommend, bake a snapshot of your case (and the Peek sample) into the page or a small JSON sidecar so it draws
  at once and then refreshes in the background.
- **Canonical login**: `TheMooJuice` and `themoojuice` are two saves and two share links. Take the login's spelling from the first API record
  (`user.login`) and store under that.
- **"Open your SPOODEX"** at the end of the reveal opens Profile, not the SPOODEX view. Either change the label or the target.
- **"Looking up 128 places…"** sits for ~10 s with a full progress bar, so the app looks frozen. Give that step its own share of the bar.
- **Display case header** names the user twice ("THEMOOJUICE'S DISPLAY CASE" over "themoojuice").
- **Possessives**: "SPIDERPALS'S SPOODEX" should read "SPIDERPALS' SPOODEX".
- **Place search** lists user-made places ("PillowFort, Austin TX", "… Home"). This is already in §11. Consider filtering to iNat's standard
  places (`admin_level` set) for home base search.
- **iPhone pass** on a real device: install to home screen, 3D map, share sheet, IndexedDB persistence after a week.

## Release decision: v2.5 or v2.6?

v2.6's Album holds up without the art. Card faces and Field art reach almost nobody while 98 of 100 artworks are untagged. Options:
(a) launch on v2.5 with the four fixes above; (b) merge v2.6 for the Album and tag the art over the following weeks. The art plumbing is
invisible until tagged, so it does no harm either way. If you tag even 15–20 of the common genera first, newcomers get a "this has
real hand-drawn art" moment, which is a strong hook for Reddit.

## Ideas

1. **Pet spood → wild spood.** Turn the captive record into the hook: "Your pet is *Phidippus regius*. Wild *Phidippus* were recorded
   within 25 km of you in the last 30 days." Show captive records on a separate "pet shelf" (not counted) and make finding the first wild one the
   first quest. This fits the core principle (close the app and go spooding), and it's aimed at your biggest audience.
2. **Launch kit**: the `og:image`; a post that links to your display case rather than the bare app; GoatCounter `?ref=reddit` (or path tag) to
   compare Reddit with the iNat forum. The iNat Forum's *Nature Talk* / *General* threads are a second, very well-matched launch channel.
3. **Image proxy** (backlog, §11): the share PNG is the viral loop, and for you and most experienced users it prints name plates. A
   Cloudflare Worker on the free tier would fix it. It would be the first backend, so treat it as a deliberate step.
4. **Monthly hunt as the community anchor**: post a 🗓 spood hunt each month on the subreddit with the existing Post text. It gives people a
   reason to return, and the leaderboard does the marketing.
5. **"Help a spood" loop for experts**: Bounty board already finds Needs ID records. An identifier mode (genera you've helped ID, as
   cards) would bring experienced people in as identifiers, which feeds everyone else's mystery spoods.
6. **Album → physical stickers**: printable blank album pages plus your sticker art suggests a printed sticker sheet. If anything is ever sold,
   note Open-Meteo's free tier is non-commercial (§3).
7. **Relative progress for low-diversity places** (§12): "16 / 29 in the UK" works. A "% of what's been found near you" line on Three to find
   would also suit people in poor salticid areas.
