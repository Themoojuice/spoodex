## Reddit (r/jumpingspiders, r/spiders, r/inaturalist)

**Title:** I made a Pokédex for jumping spiders that fills itself in from your iNaturalist photos

**Body:**

> Enter your iNaturalist username and every jumping spider genus you've photographed becomes a card: rarity by how few iNat records it has
> where you live, a map that shows what's been recorded around you that you haven't found yet, and quests built from your gaps.
> No iNat account? "See what jumping spiders live near you" works without one.
>
> Keep a pet spood? Captive records don't count towards the collection, but they get a shelf of their own in the Spoodarium, where you can
> give each one its name. There's also a terrarium to build for a spood of your own: pick a resident (some are my paintings), then kit it out
> with plants, decor, hats and bow ties that unlock as your wild finds rack up prestige. It also shows where to find a wild one of the
> genera you keep, near you.
>
> There's also a monthly spood hunt anyone can join: photograph jumping spiders anywhere this month, and the top 25 iNat observers make the
> leaderboard: [October's hunt](hunt link above).
>
> Mine, for a look: [display case](case link above). App: [SPOODEX](app link above)
>
> Free, no sign-up, runs in the browser (add it to your home screen to use it like an app). It only reads public iNat data, and your location
> never leaves your browser. Feedback welcome. I'm a jumping spider nut in Cairns and built this to get more people out spooding.

Post the app link as the **link** (so the preview image shows), and put the case and hunt links in the first comment, if the subreddit allows.

## iNaturalist Forum (Nature Talk or General)

> **SPOODEX: a collection game for jumping spiders built on iNat data**
>
> I've built a small web app that turns a user's public Salticidae observations into a genus collection: rarity by iNat record counts in
> your region, a scanner for genera recorded near you that you haven't observed, mystery-spood prompts for observations stuck above
> genus (with a photo checklist to help get them to genus), and a monthly worldwide "spood hunt" scored only from records that someone
> outside the hunt has confirmed at genus.
>
> It's read-only, uses the public API throttled to about 1 request per second per visitor, and caches in the browser. Captive observations are kept
> separate and never count. I'd value identifiers' thoughts, especially on whether the hunt's scoring encourages good records.
>
> (app link with `ref=inat-forum`)

## The monthly spood hunt

- On the 1st of each month, open Mates › Crew › **🗓 Spood hunt · <Month>** (it's the same event for everyone), press **📋 Post text**, and post
  it to the subreddit. Add `&ref=reddit` to the link.
- At the end of the month (final 7 days after it closes), post the 🪪 Result card from that event and congratulate the winners.
- The hunt is worldwide, open bioblitz scoring, no place: rarity counts the world's records.
